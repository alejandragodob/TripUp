4Full hi-fi flow in the 3a language — Newsreader titles, no emoji, and the Google Maps wishlist concept
GOOGLE MAPS — THE GROUP WISHLIST

Every trip has a wishlist: one shared pool of places. It fills itself from three sources, so the AI never picks from the whole city:

  • Connected Google Maps. Each friend connects once (Google sign-in, read-only). TripUp reads their saved lists that match the trip's city — "Lisbon wants", "Want to go" — and keeps them synced.
  • Shared links. Someone pastes a Google Maps list or place link into the trip or the group chat → auto-captured, tagged with who shared it.
  • Trip history. Places already visited, thumbs-up'd after an itinerary item completes.

Poll drafting ranks the wishlist first (saved by more people › open now › walking distance › price spread) and only falls back to Places search when the wishlist is empty. Every option carries a source line — "Saved by Maya & Theo" — so the group trusts why it's there and can swap it out.

WHAT CHANGED FROM 3a
  • Title face is now Newsreader (high-contrast editorial serif, closer to the reference); Figtree stays for UI.
  • No emoji anywhere. Icon circles hold rank numbers, times, or a simple geometric mark; the Google source mark is a plain "G" badge.
  • All ten flow screens are now hi-fi in one system: cream ground, beige feature panel, white rows with black arrow circles, outlined chips, one black pill per screen.
  • Screens 02 and 06 (★) are the two key screens for the submission.

1TripUp — the angle, the wireflow, three brand directions
THE ANGLE — DON'T FIGHT WHATSAPP, SIT INSIDE IT

Every other submission will build a better Splitwise with polls bolted on. The brief's own insight says users bail to WhatsApp after two taps. So TripUp's core object is a share-able card: every poll, expense and balance is a link that unfurls as a live preview in WhatsApp/iMessage. Voting happens without opening the app; Ren joins from a link — no invite flow, no account wall.

Partnerships that make this credible: Google Places (poll options come with distance, open-now, price), WhatsApp deep links (share & nudge), Revolut / Wise / Apple Pay for one-tap settling, Splitwise import for groups migrating a running tab. Zero new accounts: sign in with the phone number WhatsApp already knows, pay with the Apple Pay / Revolut / bank app already on the phone. TripUp adds no wallet, no balance to top up, no password.

PRODUCT PRINCIPLES (FROM THE INSIGHTS)
  • Polls are the primary verb. A trip has a "Right now" slot; empty slots invite a poll, closed polls become itinerary.
  • Two taps to anything. Poll → Expense → Settle are one sticky action bar, on every trip screen.
  • Shared ownership. Any participant can create a poll or log an expense. Organizer only differs by a small badge.
  • Debts are explained, not just computed. "Why do I pay Maya?" gets a one-line answer.
